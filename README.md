# Learning Log

A public record of a structured 12-week program to build with Claude Code: skills, apps, and agents.

I'm a senior product manager. I'm not learning to code for its own sake. I'm learning to ship working software the way I ship product: define the problem, scope tightly, build, review what happened, and adjust. This log is the review step, written down.

## Purpose

- **Hold myself accountable to shipped work.** Each session should leave something concrete behind: a repo, a tool, a decision.
- **Make the learning transferable.** Capturing what worked and what didn't turns a session into something another PM, or future me, can reuse.
- **Show the process, not just the output.** The portfolio of finished tools lives in [hello-claude](https://github.com/Taresa/hello-claude). This log documents how they got built.

## The program

Twelve weeks, three tracks, progressing in order of complexity:

1. **Skills**: reusable instructions that shape how Claude works on recurring tasks.
2. **Apps**: small, complete tools built around real practitioner workflows.
3. **Agents**: systems that take on routing, assignment, and coordination decisions.

### Scope will move

The projects planned in hello-claude are starting hypotheses, not commitments. Some will change scope as I learn what's feasible and worth building, and some may be replaced entirely along the way. When that happens, the entry for that session will say what changed and why, the same way I'd document a pivot on a product roadmap.

## How this log is organized

One file per working session, in [`entries/`](entries/), named `session-NN.md`. Entries are built around three core sections, and some add a short extra section, such as a concept explained or what's next:

| Section | Question it answers |
|---------|---------------------|
| **What I built** | What exists now that didn't before? |
| **What I learned** | What do I now understand or do differently? |
| **What I'd do differently** | If I reran the session, what would I change? |

Entries are written close to the session while the details are fresh, and they stay honest about what didn't go well.
